Keep it, and get cash now

A loan on your item, not on your credit.

A pawn loan is a short-term loan based on something you own. Bring it in, we make you an offer, and you leave with cash and a pawn ticket. There's no credit check, because the loan is based on the item.

Pay back the loan and the charges by the date on your ticket, and the item is yours again. Your ticket spells out the amount, the charges and the due date, and we'll go over it with you before you sign.

Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you check my credit?

No. A pawn loan is based on the item you bring in.

What do I need to bring?

The item and a current government photo ID.

How much can I get?

It depends on the item, its condition and what it's worth today. We'll make you an offer at the counter.

Can I pawn a gun?

Yes. To get a pawned firearm back, federal law requires you to fill out the Form 4473 and pass a background check when you pick it up, the same as buying one.

What if I can't pay it back on time?

Talk to us before the due date on your ticket. Your ticket explains your options.

Do you take tools or electronics?

No. We loan on firearms, gold and jewelry.
