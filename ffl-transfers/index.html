Ship it to us

Your gun, our counter, a few minutes of paperwork.

Good to know

Questions people ask

How much is a transfer?

Incoming transfers are $49.

Can you transfer a gun out?

No, we only take incoming transfers.

What do I give the seller?

Our license details. Call us before you order and we'll send them over, or have the seller contact us.

What do I bring to pick it up?

A current government photo ID with your address. You'll fill out the Form 4473 and pass the background check, the same as any gun purchase.

Can you transfer a suppressor?

Ask us. We sell suppressors over the counter, same day; see suppressors and SBRs.

Do I have to pick it up right away?

Come by as soon as you can during store hours, Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 7 PM.
