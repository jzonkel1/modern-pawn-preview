Privacy Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

Modern Pawn & Guns ("Modern," "we" or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what this website collects, how we use it and the choices you have.

What we collect

When you send a request through the site: your name, phone number, what you're asking about, your message and any photos you choose to attach.

When you call, text or email us: your phone number or email address and whatever you send us.

Automatically: like most websites, our web host records standard technical details such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, to keep the site secure and working.

How we use it

To answer your questions, help you buy, sell, transfer or pawn, keep the records the law requires, protect against fraud and improve this website. We don't sell your personal information.

Records we keep in the store

Federal law requires us to keep the Form 4473 and background-check records for every firearm we transfer, and Texas law requires us to record pawn loans and the items we buy, including your name, address and ID. We collect this in the store, not through this website, and share it with law enforcement only as the law requires.

Text messages

If you text us, we use your number only to reply and to talk with you about your request. We do not sell or share your mobile number or text-message consent with anyone for their marketing. Message and data rates may apply, and you can ask us to stop texting you at any time.

Cookies, analytics and maps

This site may use cookies and similar tools, such as Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use the site. These tools report things like pages viewed and general location, not your name. The maps on this site are provided by Google, which may set its own cookies.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings and opt out of Google Analytics with Google's opt-out add-on.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run this website and our business (such as our web host and the service that delivers form messages to us), government agencies and law enforcement where the law requires it, and anyone else when required by law or to protect our rights and our customers.

Other websites

Links to our online store, Google, Instagram and our neighbors Capital Coin and Afram's Jewelers take you to sites run under their own privacy policies.

Keeping it safe

We keep what you send us only as long as we need it for your request, our business records or the law, and we take reasonable steps to protect it. No website or email is perfectly secure, so please don't send card numbers or other sensitive details through the site.

Children

This site isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to stop contacting you, except for records the law requires us to keep. Texas residents have these rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act, and we'll respond within 45 days.

Changes and questions

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Questions or requests: Modern Pawn & Guns, 1701 Airline Rd, Corpus Christi, TX 78412. Call (361) 993-9390 or email modernpawn@gmail.com.
