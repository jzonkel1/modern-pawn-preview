FAQs

The short answers.

What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 7 PM. We're closed Sunday.

Where are you?

1701 Airline Road, Corpus Christi, TX 78412, just off South Padre Island Drive (SPID). Afram's Jewelers and Capital Coin are in the same building.

What do I need to buy a gun?

A current government photo ID with your address. You'll fill out the federal Form 4473 and we run the background check at the counter. Under federal law you have to be 18 to buy a rifle or shotgun from a dealer and 21 to buy a handgun.

Can I really take a suppressor home the same day?

Yes. We sell suppressors and SBRs over the counter on the Form 4473, so once your background check clears, you walk out with it. See suppressors and SBRs.

Do you do FFL transfers?

Yes, incoming transfers are $49. Call us before you order for our license details. See FFL transfers.

Do you have layaway?

Yes, generally about 20% down and paid off within 12 months. See layaway and special orders.

Can you order a gun you don't have?

Usually, yes. Tell us the make, model and caliber.

Do you buy guns?

Yes, handguns, rifles and shotguns. See sell us your gun.

What can I pawn?

Firearms, gold and jewelry. See pawn loans.

Do you buy gold?

Yes, gold chains, rings and jewelry, broken or not. See gold and jewelry.

Do you do Cerakote?

Yes, in the store, along with custom AR builds. See gunsmithing and Cerakote.

Do you have an online store?

Yes: mpguns.com. Order online and pick it up at the counter.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Come by any time during store hours.
