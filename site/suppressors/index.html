No more waiting on a stamp

Pick it out today, take it home today.

We now sell suppressors and short-barreled rifles the same way we sell any other gun. You fill out the federal form at the counter, we run the background check, and if it comes back clear, you walk out with it. No months of waiting for the can to come out of jail.

We waited until we had proper guidance from our advisors before we started, to protect our business and to protect you, our customer. If you have questions about how it works today, ask us at the counter.

We keep a lot of cans on hand from plenty of different companies, along with factory SBRs like HK's MR556. People drive in from out of town for their suppressors.

Good to know

Questions people ask

Do I still have to wait months for a suppressor?

Not here. We sell suppressors and SBRs over the counter on the Form 4473, so once your background check clears, you take it home the same day.

What do I need to bring?

A current government photo ID with your address, the same as buying any gun. If you have questions about your situation, call before you come in.

Do you have suppressors in stock?

Yes, plenty, from a lot of different companies. Call (361) 993-9390 to ask about a specific model.

Which suppressor should I get?

Tell us what you're putting it on and what you'll shoot, and we'll show you a few that fit. Most of our suppressor reviews start with “they walked me through it.”

Can you order a suppressor you don't have?

Usually, yes. See special orders.
