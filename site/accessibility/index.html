Accessibility Statement

Last updated November 1, 2026

Modern Pawn & Guns wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, voice control or larger text.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, and we check the site as we make changes to it.

What we've done

The site uses clear headings, descriptive text for images, labeled form fields, visible focus outlines, readable color contrast and layouts that work from small phones to large screens. It can be used with a keyboard alone. If your device is set to reduce motion, the moving review rows and photo rows stay still.

Content from other companies

Some features come from other companies, including our online store, Google Maps and Instagram. We can't control how accessible they are, but we're happy to help you with anything they cover.

Need help or found a problem?

If any part of this site is hard to use, call or text (361) 993-9390, email modernpawn@gmail.com or stop by the store at 1701 Airline Rd, Corpus Christi, TX 78412, Monday to Saturday, 9 to 7. We'll help you directly and work to fix the problem. Please tell us the page and what happened.
