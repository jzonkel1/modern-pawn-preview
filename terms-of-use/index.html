About this site

This website gives general information about Modern Pawn & Guns and what we do. Nothing on it is an offer, a contract or a promise that a particular item is in stock. Inventory changes daily, and prices and availability are confirmed in the store.

Buying firearms

Every firearm sale and transfer follows federal, state and local law. You'll fill out the ATF Form 4473 and pass a background check, and you must be old enough under federal law: 18 for a rifle or shotgun and 21 for a handgun from a licensed dealer. We may refuse any sale or transfer at our discretion.

Information on this site about suppressors, short-barreled rifles and other regulated items is general and may change as the law changes. It isn't legal advice. Ask us about your situation before you buy.

FFL transfers and online orders

Our online store at mpguns.com runs under its own terms. Guns ordered there or shipped to us from another seller are released only after the transfer paperwork and background check are complete.

Pawn loans

Your pawn ticket is your loan agreement. It sets out the amount, the charges, the due date and your options, and it controls over anything on this website. A pawned firearm is returned only after you complete the Form 4473 and pass a background check, as federal law requires.

Modern Pawn & Guns is licensed and examined by the Texas Office of Consumer Credit Commissioner (OCCC), 2601 N. Lamar Blvd., Austin, Texas 78705, (800) 538-1579, occc.texas.gov. Contact the OCCC with questions or complaints about a pawn transaction.

Selling to us

Offers are based on the item, its condition and the market on the day, and a sale is final once both of us agree in the store. Texas law requires us to record each purchase, including your name, address, a description of the items and your photo ID, and items we buy may be held for a period before they're resold.
